# GTT — Canonical v4

**Status:** PLANNED  
**Canonical status:** NOT YET RELEASED  
**Generation:** GTT 4  
**Purpose:** Reserved canonical definition for the GTT 4 generation.

> This document describes the anticipated long-term direction for GTT 4. It does not define capabilities as currently released GTT functionality.

---

## 1. GTT 4 Position

GTT 4 represents a future major generation of the GTT methodology.

It should build on the governed-development foundation established by earlier GTT generations while addressing the requirements of increasingly autonomous and complex AI-assisted software development.

---

## 2. Long-Term Direction

Areas that may be considered for GTT 4 include:

- advanced governance automation;
- broader multi-agent/ADE coordination;
- stronger machine-verifiable governance;
- expanded architectural intent management;
- richer lifecycle governance;
- deeper CI/CD and DevOps integration;
- stronger organizational and team-level governance;
- improved interoperability across AI development environments.

These are directional areas, not released GTT 4 capabilities.

---

## 3. GTT 4 Governance Principle

Increased automation must not be confused with increased authority.

The foundational distinction remains:

> **Agent reasoning ≠ Change authorization.**

Automation may increase while governance boundaries remain explicit.

---

## 4. Independence

GTT 4 should remain methodology-first and independent of any particular:

- AI model;
- AI agent;
- ADE;
- cloud provider;
- programming language;
- implementation technology.

---

## 5. Evolution from Previous Generations

GTT 4 should explicitly document:

- what is inherited from GTT 2.x;
- what is introduced in GTT 3;
- what is changed in GTT 4;
- what is deprecated;
- what remains compatible;
- what governance rules are strengthened.

No assumption of compatibility should be made without an explicit decision.

---

## 6. Canonical Scope

The released GTT 4 canonical definition should establish:

- GTT vision;
- principles;
- governance rules;
- capabilities/services;
- architecture;
- evidence and provenance;
- agent/ADE model;
- human authority;
- protected artifacts;
- proposals;
- lifecycle;
- CLI and implementation boundaries;
- conformance;
- terminology;
- migration from previous generations.

---

## 7. Release Boundary

**GTT 4 is not yet released.**

Nothing in this document should be presented as an existing GTT 4 capability.

When GTT 4 is formally defined and released, this planning document must be superseded by the released canonical specification.
